# Glossy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Glossy is an iOS app that turns your photo into trend-style AI photos and videos: you pick a template or write your own prompt, add a photo, and the app generates the result. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

If you have a question about this policy, write to [bakhrom86157@icloud.com](mailto:bakhrom86157@icloud.com).

## The short version

- Glossy has no accounts. You do not sign up, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
- Your photos and results are stored on your device.
- A photo leaves your device only when you start a generation: it goes to our server and to the AI provider that makes the result.
- The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
- We do not sell your information.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone:

- **Photos you choose** for generations, including the last photo you used, so you can reuse it.
- **Your results:** the photos and videos the app generated for you, with the template or prompt used.
- **Prompts you write** in the "Own prompt" mode.
- **App data:** your credit balance, subscription status, rating you gave the app, onboarding progress and similar settings.
- **A random installation identifier** created on first launch. It is not tied to your name, email address or Apple ID.

This information stays on your device unless one of the features described below sends part of it out. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include the app's data in those backups under Apple's own terms.

## Information that leaves your device

### AI generation

Before the first generation the app asks for your permission to share your photo with the AI provider; nothing is sent until you agree, and you can decline. When you start a generation, the app sends the data that generation needs to our server, which passes it on to our AI processing provider, kie.ai. The key to the AI service is kept on the server, never in the app. The data sent is:

- the photo you chose for it;
- the text instruction for the chosen template, or the prompt you wrote yourself;
- for some video templates, a preparatory image made from your photo in an earlier step.

For the AI model to read a photo, the photo is stored on our server and the AI provider downloads it from there. Generated photos and videos are copied from the provider to our server, so the app can show them. These files are reachable through long, hard-to-guess web addresses that are not published anywhere. The AI provider may keep its own temporary copies for a limited time.

The AI provider runs image and video models made by Google, ByteDance, Kuaishou (Kling) and MiniMax (Hailuo) and processes this data on our behalf to return the result. Its own handling of data is described in its privacy policy at [kie.ai](https://kie.ai). We do not use your photos to train AI models.

Photos of your face are personal, and you decide whether to share them. Upload only photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed to it.

### Our server

Our server keeps:

- the photos you upload for a generation, for 24 hours, after which they are deleted automatically;
- the photos and videos generated for you, for 30 days, after which they are deleted automatically;
- a random token that identifies your installation of the app, created the first time you start a generation, together with the installation identifier and the internet address it was registered from, to limit abuse. It is not tied to your name, email address or Apple ID;
- a record of each generation (the AI model used, its status and time), for 30 days, to enforce usage limits.

Server request logs, which include internet addresses, are kept for a short time for troubleshooting.

### Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are bought through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card number or billing details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

To check whether your subscription is active and to show prices, the app uses Adapty, a subscription management service. Adapty receives the random installation identifier, your purchase and subscription history from Apple, basic device information (such as the iOS version, device model, language and region) and your internet address. Adapty's handling of data is described in its [privacy policy](https://adapty.io/privacy).

### Ratings

After your first finished result the app may show Apple's standard review prompt, and the "Rate Glossy" button opens the App Store review page. Ratings and reviews go to Apple and are handled under Apple's terms; the app does not see them.

## Permissions

- **Photo library:** to choose a photo for a generation.
- **Add to Photos:** to save your results to Photos.
- **Camera:** to take a selfie for a generation.

Each permission is requested when you first use the feature that needs it. You can change any of them later in iOS Settings.

## How we use information

Information is used only to provide the app's features: generating your photos and videos, keeping your results and credits, checking your purchases and preventing abuse. We do not use it for advertising and do not build profiles of you for third parties.

## Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named above, and only as far as each feature requires. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Data on your device stays there until you delete it. You can delete individual results in the "Mine" tab.
- Deleting the app removes all data the app stored on the device.
- Uploaded photos are deleted from our server after 24 hours, and results after 30 days. Save the results you want to keep to Photos.

Because the app has no accounts, we cannot link the files on our server to you, look them up or restore them. To have them deleted earlier, write to us and include the date of the generation.

## Children

Glossy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has uploaded their photos to the app, contact us at the address below.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Most of this you can do directly in the app, since the data is on your device. For anything else, write to us and we will respond within 30 days.

## Security

Data sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International transfers

The service providers named above may process data in countries other than the one you live in.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be noted in the app or in the release notes.
